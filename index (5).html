About

Who I Am

I am a first-year PhD researcher at the University of Geneva, working under the supervision of Arnaud Casteigts. My research focuses on temporal graphs — dynamic structures where edges are present only at certain times — with particular interest in reachability, connectivity, and complexity in time-varying networks.

I hold an ESKAS (Swiss Government Excellence) scholarship funding my doctoral research in Switzerland. Before my PhD, I worked on problems at the intersection of algorithms and fairness in computational social choice, and I hold a broader interest in cryptography.
